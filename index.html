Getting to Laino Borgo

Plan: AMS → Laino Borgo via Naples + train, maximizing the direct flight from Amsterdam.

✈️ Flight

  • Amsterdam (AMS) → Naples (NAP), direct, ~2h40
  • KLM, easyJet or Transavia — all daily in late April
  • From the airport: Alibus shuttle to Napoli Centrale (~20 min)

🚆 Train

  • Napoli Centrale → Sapri, the last coastal station before Calabria (~50 km from Laino Borgo)
  • Intercity ~1h50 or regional ~2h15 — both take bikes with a €3.50 supplement
  • On the Intercity, the bike slot must be reserved when booking; on regional trains just pick a train marked with the bike symbol

🚲 Last leg — Sapri → Laino Borgo (~50 km)

  • Option A Ride it — a ~900 m warm-up climb into Calabria
  • Option B Taxi, ~50 min, roughly €80–100
